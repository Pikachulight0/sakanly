## 📌 Project Overview

**Sakanly** is a housing platform designed to help students and fresh graduates find suitable and affordable accommodation based on **location, budget, preferences, and lifestyle**.

Finding a place to live is one of the first big challenges for students and new graduates. Listings are scattered, hard to compare, and often missing important details. Sakanly brings housing options together in one place and makes it easier to **search, filter, compare, save, and explore** them through a clear and simple experience.

### 🎯 Target Users

| User | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 🎓 **Students** | University students looking for housing close to their campus that fits their budget. |
| 💼 **Fresh Graduates** | Recent graduates relocating for work or study who need affordable housing. |
| 🏠 **Housing Providers** | Owners or managers who want to publish housing listings and communicate with seekers. |

### 👤 User Roles

| Role | Main Capabilities |
|------|-------------------|
| **Housing Seeker** | Register, log in, search and filter housing, view details, compare options, save favorites, rate and review, contact providers, manage profile. |
| **Housing Provider** | Register, log in, publish and manage housing listings, add photos and amenities, communicate with seekers. |

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. **Make housing search easier and faster** by offering clear search and filtering tools.
2. **Help students and fresh graduates find affordable accommodation** that matches their budget and needs.
3. **Reduce the difficulty of comparing housing options** by letting users compare listings side by side.
4. **Provide organized and reliable housing information**, including details, photos, amenities, and location.
5. **Improve communication** between housing seekers and housing providers.
6. **Create a simple, accessible, and user-friendly experience** designed especially for students and fresh graduates.

## 📦 Project Scope

### 👤 User Features

- Create an account and log in.
- Manage a personal profile.
- Browse housing listings and view detailed information.
- View housing photos and amenities.

### 🏠 Housing Provider Features

- Register and log in as a housing provider.
- Publish housing listings with details, photos, price, location, and amenities.
- Manage published listings.
- Respond to messages from housing seekers.

### 🔎 Search & Discovery

- Search for available housing.
- Filter by **location**, **price**, **housing type**, and **preferences**.
- Compare different housing options side by side.

### 💬 Communication

- Allow housing seekers to contact housing providers about a listing.
- Allow providers to reply to seekers.

### ⭐ Reviews & Favorites

- Rate and review housing listings.
- Save favorite listings and view them later.

### 📍 Location & Maps

- Show the housing location on a map.
- Help users understand where a listing is relative to their area of interest.

### 🎨 UX/UI

- Simple, clean, and easy-to-navigate interface.
- Designed with students and fresh graduates in mind.
- Consistent design system and a clickable prototype.

### 🚫 Out of Scope

To keep the project realistic for a five-week graduation timeline, the following are **not** part of the current scope:

- Online payment systems
- Advanced analytics or enterprise-level administration tools
- Native mobile applications
